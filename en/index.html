Before you call

What does the $50 first visit include?

The practice’s website lists a new patient exam and X-rays for $50.

Do you take my insurance?

The office accepts most insurance, including Delta Dental, Cigna, Aetna, Guardian, Anthem, MetLife, United Concordia, Principal and Medi-Cal (Denti-Cal). Plans change, so call and we’ll check yours.

Can I bring my kids?

Yes. Children’s dentistry is part of our family practice, so the whole family can be seen in one place.

Is the staff bilingual?

We serve patients in English and Spanish, and our patient forms come in both languages.
